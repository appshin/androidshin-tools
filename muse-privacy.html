Privacy Policy

Muse: Poems & Calm Music (영감) · package com.appshin.muse · Developer: Mr.AndroidShin
Effective date: October 7, 2026

English한국어

1. Summary

Muse reads original poems aloud with background music. It works offline, has no user accounts, and the developer does not run any server that receives your data. The only data that leaves your device is handled by Google services used for advertising, purchases and app updates, described below.

2. Data stored only on your device

The app saves a few settings on your device: selected language and mood, reading speed, whether ads were removed, and the date of first launch (used so that full-screen ads are not shown on the first day). This data is never sent to the developer. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Permissions

Internet (ads, purchases, updates), notifications and foreground playback (to keep reading with the screen off and show playback controls). The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Your choices

  • Change ad consent at any time: app Settings → Privacy & ad settings (shown where required).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove all ads permanently with the one-time “Remove ads” purchase.
  • Delete locally stored settings: clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

6. Children

Muse is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security and retention

The developer does not collect or store your personal data. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's policies.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

1. 요약

영감은 창작시를 배경음악과 함께 낭송해 주는 앱입니다. 오프라인으로 동작하며 회원가입·로그인이 없고, 개발자는 사용자 데이터를 받는 서버를 운영하지 않습니다. 기기 밖으로 나가는 데이터는 아래의 광고·결제·업데이트용 Google 서비스가 처리하는 정보뿐입니다.

2. 기기에만 저장되는 정보

선택한 언어와 분위기, 낭송 속도, 광고 제거 여부, 최초 실행일(첫날 전면 광고를 표시하지 않기 위함)을 기기에만 저장합니다. 이 정보는 개발자에게 전송되지 않으며, 앱 데이터 삭제 또는 앱 삭제 시 함께 삭제됩니다.
