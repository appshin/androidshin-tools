Privacy Policy — World Coin Shooter

App: World Coin Shooter (package com.appshin.coinshooter)
Developer: Mr.AndroidShin
Effective date: October 4, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 4, 2026

Summary. World Coin Shooter is a casual arcade game. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location, or any directly identifying personal information. The game stores only your in-app settings on your own device. To show ads and provide core services, the app uses Google services (Google AdMob and Google Play services), which may collect certain device and advertising identifiers as described below.

1. Information the app stores on your device

The following preferences are saved locally on your device only (Android SharedPreferences). They are not transmitted to us and are removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Selected country and interface language
  • Sound and music on/off settings
  • Your best score and highest level reached

We (the developer) do not have access to this data and do not collect it.

2. Information collected by third-party services

To operate the game we use the following third-party services, which may collect data automatically:

Google AdMob (advertising)

The app displays banner and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (non-precise) location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, network)
  • Ad interaction and app-usage events needed for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention

This data is handled under Google's privacy policy. For details, see Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and AdMob & privacy.

Google Play services & Google Play Core

The app uses Google Play services and the Google Play In-App Update library (to check for and install app updates) and Google Play Billing is not used. These components may process device and diagnostic information as part of their normal operation, governed by Google's privacy policy linked above.

3. How the data is used and shared

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Permissions used

  • INTERNET — required to load ads and check for app updates.
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — allows access to the Android Advertising ID for advertising.

5. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads), where you can reset the ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Remove all locally stored data: uninstalling the app deletes all settings and scores saved on your device.

6. Children

World Coin Shooter is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app shows advertising via AdMob, it is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us and we will address it.

7. Data retention & security

Settings and scores remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google is retained and secured according to Google's policies. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we rely on reputable providers (Google) to safeguard the data they process.

8. International users

The app is available worldwide. Data processed by Google may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact: support@androidshin.dev (Mr.AndroidShin).

1. 기기에 저장되는 정보

다음 설정은 사용자의 기기에만 로컬로 저장되며(안드로이드 SharedPreferences), 개발자에게 전송되지 않고 앱을 삭제하면 함께 제거됩니다.

  • 선택한 국가 및 표시 언어

  • 최고 점수 및 최고 도달 단계

개발자는 위 데이터에 접근하지 않으며 수집하지 않습니다.

2. 제3자 서비스가 수집하는 정보

게임 운영을 위해 아래의 제3자 서비스를 사용하며, 이들은 데이터를 자동으로 수집할 수 있습니다.

Google AdMob (광고)

앱은 Google AdMob을 통해 배너 및 보상형 광고를 표시합니다. 광고 제공·측정을 위해 Google 및 파트너가 다음을 수집·처리할 수 있습니다.

  • 광고 식별자(Android 광고 ID)
  • IP 주소 및 이를 통한 대략적인(정밀하지 않은) 위치
  • 기기 정보(모델, 운영체제, 언어, 네트워크)
  • 광고 전달·노출 제한·부정 방지를 위한 광고 상호작용 및 앱 사용 이벤트

해당 데이터는 Google 개인정보처리방침에 따라 처리됩니다. 자세한 내용은 Google 개인정보처리방침, Google 서비스를 사용하는 사이트·앱의 정보 이용 방식, AdMob과 개인정보 보호를 참고하세요.

Google Play 서비스 & Google Play Core

앱은 Google Play 서비스와 Google Play 인앱 업데이트 라이브러리(업데이트 확인·설치용)를 사용하며, Google Play 결제(Billing)는 사용하지 않습니다. 이 구성요소는 정상 동작을 위해 기기·진단 정보를 처리할 수 있으며, 위에 링크된 Google 정책의 적용을 받습니다.
