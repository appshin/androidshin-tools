Snaplore Privacy Policy

App: Snaplore · Package: com.appshin.snaplore · Developer: Mr.AndroidShin
Effective date: November 19, 2026

English한국어

Snaplore ("the app") lets you share a short video link (for example from YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels or TikTok) and turns it into an organized note with AI. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Snaplore is not affiliated with YouTube, Instagram or TikTok.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • The app never downloads video files.
  • Your notes, screenshots and thumbnails are stored only on your device.
  • To create an AI note, the link's public information (and screenshots you choose to add) is sent to our server and to our AI provider for processing. We do not store that content on our server.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share data with

  • Anthropic, PBC (Claude AI) — processes the link information and screenshots you submit to generate the note. Anthropic processes API data under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models by default.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosts our server (Workers and KV storage) that holds the anonymous credit account and forwards AI requests.
  • Google LLC — Google Play Billing (payments), Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (ads and consent).
  • Video platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or the site of the link you share) — your device requests the public page or oEmbed information of the link you share, so that platform receives a normal web request from your device.

We may disclose information if required by law. We do not sell or rent personal data.

4. Security

All communication between the app and our server, Anthropic, Google and Cloudflare uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Server access is restricted.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Delete any clip in the app (Clip → Delete); its note, thumbnail and screenshots are removed from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • To delete your anonymous credit account from our server, email us your credit code (Settings → My credit code). Note that remaining credits cannot be restored after deletion.
  • IP-based counters expire automatically after about 25 hours.

6. Children

Snaplore is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent for personalized ads. Contact us at the address below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
