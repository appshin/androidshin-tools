Lantern Orbit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lantern Orbit (package com.appshin.lanternorbit), published by Mr.AndroidShin (“we”), handles information. We do not operate accounts or our own servers for this app, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

1. Information stored only on your device

Best score, star shards, owned and equipped skins, sound/music/vibration settings, selected language, ad-frequency counters and a cached “ads removed” flag are saved locally on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads). AdMob may collect and process the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interaction data and approximate location derived from the IP address, in order to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice at any time with “Privacy options” in the main menu. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

Optional purchases (Remove ads, star shard packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your name, e-mail address or payment card details; the app only receives the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play to deliver the item.

4. In-app updates — Google Play

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This check is handled by the Google Play Store under Google's privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — loading ads, purchases and update checks
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback (can be turned off in the menu)
  • AD_ID — advertising ID used by Google AdMob
  • BILLING — Google Play in-app purchases

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the third-party services above: Google AdMob and Google Play (Google LLC), under the Google Privacy Policy. Data handled by these SDKs is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data stays until you clear app data or uninstall. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its policies. You may contact us at any time with questions or requests about your data.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, CCPA or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled by clearing the app's data, using “Privacy options” in the app, or contacting Google directly.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
