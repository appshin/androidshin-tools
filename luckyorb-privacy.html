Lucky Orb privacy policy

App: Lucky Orb (럭키오브 645) · Package: com.appshin.luckyorb · Effective: November 6, 2026

English한국어

Lucky Orb is a number generator and tracker for Lotto 6/45 and US Powerball, made for fun. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and who it is shared with. We built the app so that your personal inputs stay on your device.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in. We do not run our own server and do not receive your personal information.
  • Your date of birth, dream text, number preferences and saved numbers are stored only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Purchases and app updates are handled by Google Play.
  • The app downloads public lottery results. These requests contain no personal information.

Data stored only on your device

DataPurpose
Date of birth (solar or lunar), favorite, must-include and excluded numbers, pattern settingsTo build your personal combinations
Dream text and dream keywords you enterMatched against a built-in keyword list on your device to suggest numbers
Saved numbers (including numbers read from ticket QR codes)To show your picks and check results after each draw
Settings (language, game, sound, vibration, intro video)To remember your preferences

This data is never uploaded to us or to any third party. It may be included in Android's own device backup if you have backup enabled in your Google account; that backup is managed by Google under its own terms. You can delete saved numbers in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

Camera

The camera is used only when you open "QR ticket check" to scan the QR code on a Korean Lotto 6/45 ticket. Images are analyzed on your device by Google ML Kit and are not saved or sent anywhere. Only the numbers read from the code are stored, and only if you choose to save them.

Internet access

The app downloads public draw results from GitHub (an open dataset of Korean Lotto results) and from the New York State open data portal (data.ny.gov, Powerball results). Like any web request, these servers receive your IP address and basic request information; we do not control or receive that data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform is shown first. Learn more at Google's advertising policy and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying Premium removes ads.

Purchases and app updates (Google Play)

The one-time Premium purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details; the app only checks whether Premium is owned. In-app update checks are performed through Google Play.

Who data is shared with

  • Google (AdMob, Play Billing, Play in-app updates, ML Kit on-device scanning) as described above.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone else.

Age

The app is not directed to children. It is intended for adults who meet the legal age to buy lottery tickets where they live (19 in Korea; 18 or older in most US states). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete saved numbers inside the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove all local data.
  • Deny or revoke camera permission in Android settings; everything except QR scanning keeps working.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or purchase Premium to remove ads.

Security

Data stays inside the app's private storage on your device. Network requests for draw results use HTTPS.

Important notice

Lucky Orb does not predict or guarantee wins; every combination has the same odds. The app does not sell lottery tickets or buy them for you, and is not affiliated with any lottery operator.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version. Questions: contact@androidshin.dev (Mr.AndroidShin).

카메라

카메라는 'QR 당첨확인'을 열어 로또 6/45 용지의 QR 코드를 읽을 때만 사용합니다. 영상은 Google ML Kit가 기기 안에서 분석하며 저장하거나 전송하지 않습니다. 코드에서 읽은 번호만, 사용자가 저장을 선택한 경우에 저장됩니다.

인터넷 사용

공개 당첨번호를 GitHub(한국 로또 결과 공개 데이터셋)와 뉴욕주 공공데이터 포털(data.ny.gov, 파워볼 결과)에서 내려받습니다. 일반적인 웹 요청과 마찬가지로 해당 서버는 IP 주소 등 기본 요청 정보를 받게 되며, 개발자는 이 정보를 관리하거나 전달받지 않습니다.

광고 (Google AdMob)

무료 버전에는 Google AdMob의 배너, 전면, 선택형 보상형 광고가 표시됩니다. AdMob은 광고 표시·측정·빈도 제한과 부정행위 방지를 위해 광고 ID, IP 주소, 기기·앱 정보, 광고 상호작용 정보를 수집·이용할 수 있습니다. 법적으로 필요한 지역(예: 유럽경제지역, 영국)에서는 Google User Messaging Platform의 동의 화면이 먼저 표시됩니다. 자세한 내용은 Google 광고 정책과 Google 개인정보처리방침을 참고하세요. 광고 ID는 Android 설정에서 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있으며, 프리미엄을 구매하면 광고가 제거됩니다.

결제와 앱 업데이트 (Google Play)

프리미엄 1회 결제는 Google Play 결제 시스템이 처리합니다. 개발자는 결제 정보를 받지 않으며, 앱은 프리미엄 보유 여부만 확인합니다. 인앱 업데이트 확인은 Google Play를 통해 이루어집니다.
