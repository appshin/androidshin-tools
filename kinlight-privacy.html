3. 제3자 서비스

  • Kakao Corp. – 카카오 로그인 및 카카오톡 메시지 전송. 전송된 메시지는 사용자 본인의 카카오 계정에 보관되며 카카오 개인정보처리방침을 따릅니다.
  • Google AdMob – 보호자 설정 화면 하단 광고(어르신 홈 화면에는 광고 없음). 광고 ID 등 기기 정보가 처리될 수 있으며, 유럽 등에서는 동의 화면(UMP)을 표시합니다. Google 개인정보처리방침
  • Google Play 결제 – Kinlight Plus 구독 처리. 결제 정보는 Google이 처리하며 개발자는 받지 않습니다.
  • Google Play 서비스 – 위치 제공, 신체 활동 인식, 인앱 업데이트.

개발자는 위 서비스 외의 제3자에게 정보를 판매하거나 제공하지 않습니다.

Summary
Kinlight has no developer server. Locations are sent only from the senior's phone to their own KakaoTalk “Chat with myself”. The developer (Mr.AndroidShin) does not collect, store or view any personal data, including location.

1. Who we are

Kinlight is provided by independent developer Mr.AndroidShin (“developer”).
Contact: contact@androidshin.dev

3. Third-party services

  • Kakao Corp. – Kakao Login and KakaoTalk messaging. Messages are kept in the user's own Kakao account under Kakao's privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob – a banner on the guardian settings screen only (no ads on the senior's home screen). Device data such as the advertising ID may be processed; a consent form (UMP) is shown where required. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing – Kinlight Plus subscriptions. Payment data is handled by Google, not the developer.
  • Google Play services – location, activity recognition and in-app updates.

The developer does not sell or share data with any other third party.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Data on the device is deleted immediately when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared (Settings › Apps › Kinlight › Storage › Clear data).
  • Messages sent to KakaoTalk can be deleted by the user in their own KakaoTalk.
  • To disconnect Kakao: KakaoTalk › Settings › Kakao Account › Connected services › Kinlight.

5. Consent and your rights

  • Location sharing starts only after consent from the user or their legal guardian on the first-run consent screen.
  • A notification is always shown while sending, and sending can be stopped at any time from the guardian menu.
  • Location, activity, phone and notification permissions can be revoked at any time in the phone's settings.
  • The app never runs hidden and never hides its icon.

6. Children

Kinlight is designed for older adults and their families and is not directed to children.

7. Safety notice

Kinlight does not replace emergency services or medical devices. In an emergency, call your local emergency number first. Location accuracy depends on the phone's GPS and network conditions.
