CardWave Privacy Policy

App: CardWave (명함웨이브) · Package: com.appshin.cardwave · Developer: Mr.AndroidShin · Effective date: November 7, 2026

English한국어
In short: your business cards, notes and network data are stored only on your device. There is no sign-up, we do not sell data, and we never collect your location. Data leaves your device only when you choose to send your card, or through the Google services listed below (ads, purchases, optional online QR link).

1. Who we are

CardWave is developed and operated by Mr.AndroidShin (“we”). Contact: contact@androidshin.dev

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is created by you and stored locally in the app’s private storage. We do not have access to it.

  • Your own business card(s): photos of the front/back, the merged card image, and the text you enter or that is recognized (name, title, company, department, phone, email, website, address).
  • Cards you receive or scan from other people, with tags, notes, event names, favorites, meeting history and follow-up reminders.
  • App settings (language, theme, sound, event mode, purchase status).

Deleting a card in the app, clearing the app’s data, or uninstalling the app permanently removes this data from your device.

4. When you share your card

Sending your card is always started by you. CardWave hands your card image and contact text to the method you choose:

  • Nearby swipe: sent directly to the other phone over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi. No server is involved.
  • Text message (SMS/MMS), Quick Share, KakaoTalk or other apps: CardWave opens the app you choose with your card attached. Delivery is handled by that app and is subject to its own privacy policy. The recipient’s phone number you type is only used to pre-fill the message (it is also placed on your clipboard for convenience) and is not stored by CardWave.
  • Contact QR (offline): your contact details are encoded only inside the QR code shown on your screen.
  • Image link QR (optional, online): if enabled, the card image you choose is uploaded to Google Firebase Storage under an anonymous identifier, and your contact details are placed inside the link itself. Anyone who has the link can view and download that card image. You can ask us to delete it at any time (section 8).

Cards you receive from other people are stored only on your device.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not collect or track your location.
  • We do not upload your card collection, notes or network map to any server.
  • We do not require an account.

7. Security

Data on your device is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox. Network communication with Google services uses HTTPS.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any card, note or reminder in the app at any time; uninstalling removes all local data.
  • Reset or limit ad personalization in your device’s Google settings, or remove ads with Pro.
  • To delete a card image uploaded through an image link QR, or to exercise rights under laws such as the GDPR, Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act or the CCPA, email contact@androidshin.dev with the link. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children

CardWave is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be updated and significant changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
