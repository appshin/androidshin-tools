Pictell Privacy Policy

App: Pictell: Find Photos by Words · Package: com.appshin.pictell · Developer: Mr.AndroidShin
Effective date: November 19, 2026
In short: Pictell analyzes your photos on your device. Your photos, the analysis results, your search text and the words you teach the app are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We do not operate any server that receives your data. Some features rely on Google services (ads, purchases, address lookup, speech input), which are described below.

1. Data processed on your device

  • Photos and their metadata (date taken, GPS coordinates saved in the photo, file name, size). Pictell reads them with your permission to build a local search index.
  • Analysis results: scene and object labels, the number of faces and whether people are smiling, colors, and text recognized in photos (OCR), plus numeric image features used to find similar photos. Face detection only counts faces; Pictell does not identify people or create facial recognition templates.
  • Words you teach: when you teach a new word, the example images you pick are converted into numeric features and small thumbnails, stored inside the app.
  • Search history, saved searches and settings (language, theme, sound, etc.).

All of this stays in the app's private storage. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can also delete taught words and search history inside the app, and re-run analysis at any time.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your photos or personal information on any server of ours.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • We do not require an account or login.

4. Permissions

  • Photos and media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / selected photos): to analyze and search your photos.
  • Photo location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): to read where a photo was taken from its metadata. Pictell does not access your current location.
  • Notifications: to show progress while photos are being analyzed in the background.
  • Internet / Advertising ID: for ads, purchases, updates, address lookup and shared links.

5. Children

Pictell is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

Your data stays in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot access. Network connections made by Google services use encryption.

7. Your choices

  • Revoke photo access at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Pictell › Permissions.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, and change ad consent where the consent form is offered.
  • Delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
